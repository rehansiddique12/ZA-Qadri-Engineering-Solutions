Why Choose Our Fire Protection Services

Benefits of a Professional Fire Fighting System

Fire is one of the greatest risks facing any industrial or commercial facility. A properly designed and maintained fire fighting system is not a luxury - it is a legal requirement and a critical investment in the safety of your people and assets. With ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions, you get fire protection systems built to international standards and installed by trained professionals.

  • NFPA Compliant Design: Every system follows NFPA 20, NFPA 13, NFPA 14 and NFPA 72 requirements for maximum safety and reliability.
  • Complete Turnkey Service: Design, equipment supply, installation, testing and maintenance - all from a single contractor.
  • Trusted by Major Clients: We have installed fire systems for PPL Karachi, the Navy and numerous industrial plants across Pakistan.
  • Certified Equipment: We supply certified fire pumps, valves, detectors, panels and sprinkler heads from reputable international brands.
  • Quick Response: Trained installation crews and proper planning ensure minimum downtime for your operations.
  • Ongoing Support: Regular inspection, testing and maintenance keep your fire fighting system always ready for an emergency.

We understand that every facility is different. Our team performs a detailed site survey, hydraulic calculation and hazard assessment before recommending the right mix of hydrants, sprinklers and suppression systems for your specific risk profile. The result is a fire safety solution that protects life and property while meeting all regulatory requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fire Fighting System FAQs

  • What is a fire fighting system?
    A fire fighting system is a combination of equipment and piping designed to detect, control and extinguish fires. It typically includes fire pumps, hydrants, sprinklers, fire alarm panels, detectors and suppression systems that protect life and property.
  • Which fire fighting system is right for my building?
    The right system depends on the building type, occupancy, hazard class and available water supply. Our engineers survey your site and recommend hydrant, sprinkler, alarm and suppression systems per NFPA codes and local regulations.
  • Do you install fire fighting systems in factories and industrial plants?
    Yes. We install complete fire fighting systems in factories, industrial plants, warehouses, chemical facilities, high-rise buildings and commercial complexes. Our clients include PPL Karachi, the Navy and major industrial plants across Pakistan.
  • What standards do you follow for fire fighting installation?
    All our fire fighting systems are designed and installed in accordance with NFPA standards including NFPA 20, NFPA 13, NFPA 14, NFPA 72 and NFPA 10, ensuring safety, reliability and code compliance.
  • Do you provide maintenance and testing of fire systems?
    Yes. We offer regular inspection, testing and maintenance services including pump testing, hydrostatic testing, detector testing and alarm panel servicing to keep your fire fighting system fully operational.
  • Which cities do you serve for fire fighting services?
    We provide fire fighting system installation and maintenance in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all major industrial cities across Pakistan.
