Solar Structure Fabrication & Installation in Pakistan

A strong, correctly installed mounting structure is essential for the performance and safety of any solar power system. ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions fabricates and installs solar structures across Pakistan for rooftop, ground-mount and carport solar projects.

We design each structure to withstand wind and dead loads, fabricate using hot-dip galvanised steel, and install with precise orientation and tilt angles for maximum energy yield. Whether you are a commercial, industrial or residential client, our team delivers solar mounting solutions that are durable, cost-effective and built to last. Get in touch for solar structure design, fabrication and installation services.

From single-line rooftop layouts to multi-megawatt ground-mount farms, we deliver complete solar mounting solutions with accurate shop drawings and certified installation teams. Contact us for solar structure fabrication quotations anywhere in Pakistan.

Why Choose Our Solar Structures

Benefits of Professional Solar Mounting Systems

A solar power plant is only as reliable as the structure that carries it. Poorly designed mounting systems can sag, corrode or even collapse under wind load, damaging expensive panels. Our structures are engineered and galvanized to give you decades of trouble-free performance.

  • Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel: Superior corrosion resistance means a maintenance-free life of 25+ years.
  • Engineered for Wind Load: Structures are designed for the wind and weather conditions of your specific site.
  • Maximum Energy Yield: Correct tilt angles and row spacing capture the most sunlight throughout the year.
  • Versatile Designs: Ground mount, roof mount, carport and elevated options for any location or capacity.
  • Quick Installation: Precision-fabricated components bolt together fast, reducing on-site labour and time.
  • Complete Turnkey Service: Site survey, foundation, structure, panel mounting and finishing under one contract.

We work closely with solar developers, EPC contractors and direct consumers to deliver structures that fit the exact site layout and module type. Our engineering team provides complete drawings and load calculations, and our installation crews execute the project safely, on schedule and to the highest quality standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Solar Structure FAQs

  • What types of solar structures do you fabricate?
    We fabricate ground mount structures, roof mount structures, solar carports and elevated structures for solar power plants, in fixed tilt and single-axis configurations to suit any site and capacity.
  • What material is used for solar mounting structures?
    We use structural steel sections that are hot-dip galvanized for maximum corrosion resistance, along with high-quality aluminum and stainless steel fasteners, ensuring 25+ years of service life.
  • Can you install solar structures on rooftops?
    Yes. We design and install rooftop solar structures that are lightweight, wind-resistant and roof-friendly, using custom frames and mounting kits suited to RCC and metal roofs.
  • Do you provide site surveys before installation?
    Yes. Our team performs a complete site survey including soil and ground assessment, wind and shading analysis and orientation study before recommending the best structure design for your project.
  • Do you install solar structures across Pakistan?
    Yes. We provide solar structure fabrication and installation services in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all provinces of Pakistan for residential, commercial and utility-scale projects.
  • Can you supply structures for large solar power plants?
    Yes. We fabricate and supply structural systems for MW-scale solar power plants with complete engineering support, including layouts, foundation details and full installation on site.
