Message From
ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions CEO

Welcome to ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions. It is my pleasure to welcome you to our company, where engineering excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction are the foundation of everything we do. Since our establishment, we have remained committed to delivering reliable engineering and contracting solutions that contribute to the growth of industries and infrastructure across Pakistan.

At ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions, every project is more than just a contract it is a commitment built on trust, quality, and professionalism. Our experienced team specializes in PEB steel structures, industrial piping systems, fire fighting systems, insulation and cladding, sandblasting, painting, waterproofing, solar structures, and storage tank fabrication. We strive to deliver every project with precision, efficiency, and the highest standards of workmanship.

On behalf of the entire ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions family, I extend my sincere gratitude to our valued clients, business partners, and employees for their continued trust and confidence. We remain dedicated to building long-term relationships and delivering innovative engineering solutions that create lasting value. We look forward to serving you with professionalism, integrity, and excellence for many years to come.

Chief Executive Officer
