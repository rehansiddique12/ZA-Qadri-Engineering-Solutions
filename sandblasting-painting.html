Sandblasting & Painting Services in Pakistan

Surface preparation is the most important step in any protective coating system. ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions provides industrial sandblasting and painting services in Pakistan that extend the life of steel structures, tanks, pipes and equipment.

We use abrasive blasting to achieve the required surface profile and cleanliness, followed by high-performance primer, intermediate and finish coats applied by trained applicators. Our coating systems are selected for atmospheric, immersion and chemical exposure conditions to prevent corrosion and give structures a long, maintenance-free life. From small components to large structural steelwork, we deliver quality surface finishing with proper inspection at every stage.

Why Choose Our Blasting & Coating Services

Benefits of Professional Surface Treatment

Corrosion is the number one enemy of steel in Pakistan's humid coastal and industrial environments. A high-quality blasting and coating job protects your investment for decades, while a poor job fails within months. With ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions, every project is prepared to the correct cleanliness and roughness grade, then coated with the right system for the service environment.

  • Correct Surface Preparation: Blasting to SSPC and ISO 8501 grades removes all rust, scale and old coatings for perfect adhesion.
  • Long Lasting Protection: Anti-corrosion systems including zinc rich primer and epoxy give decades of asset protection.
  • Standards Compliance: Work follows SSPC, NACE and ISO 8501 specifications with DFT measurement and inspection on every job.
  • Experienced Crews: Trained blasters, painters and inspectors deliver consistent, high-quality finish every time.
  • Workshop & On-Site: We blast and paint in our workshop or at your site with controlled containment and environmental care.
  • Full Coating Systems: Primer, intermediate and topcoat applied as engineered systems for maximum durability and finish.

From small fabricated components to entire structural steel frames, tanks and vessels, we manage the complete coating cycle - blasting, inspection, priming, painting and final verification. We keep proper records of surface profile, humidity, temperature and dry film thickness so you have full documentation for your quality department and auditors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sandblasting & Painting FAQs

  • What is industrial sandblasting?
    Industrial sandblasting, also called abrasive blasting, is the process of propelling abrasive media at high velocity to clean and profile steel surfaces. It removes rust, old paint and mill scale to prepare the surface for protective coatings.
  • Why is surface preparation important before painting?
    Surface preparation is the most important step in any coating system. A properly cleaned and profiled surface allows the paint to grip firmly, preventing rust, peeling and premature coating failure and extending the life of the structure.
  • Which coating systems do you apply?
    We apply industrial coating systems including zinc rich primers, epoxy coatings, polyurethane (PU) topcoats and anti-corrosion coatings, selected and applied per SSPC and NACE standards for the service environment.
  • Can you sandblast and paint steel structures, tanks and equipment?
    Yes. We provide blasting and coating for steel structures, storage tanks, pipes, vessels, cranes and heavy equipment, both in our workshop and on site across industrial facilities in Pakistan.
  • What standards do you follow for blasting and coating?
    Our work follows internationally recognised standards including SSPC surface preparation standards, NACE coating specifications and ISO 8501 cleanliness grades, with dry film thickness measured and documented on every job.
  • Which cities do you serve for sandblasting and painting?
    We provide sandblasting and industrial painting services in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all major industrial cities across Pakistan.
