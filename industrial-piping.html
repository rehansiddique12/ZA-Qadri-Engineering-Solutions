Industrial Piping Services in Pakistan

Industrial piping is the backbone of every processing plant, and a poorly executed piping system can shut down an entire production line. ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions provides end-to-end industrial piping services in Pakistan, from line design and material procurement to fabrication, welding, testing and commissioning.

Our welders and pipefitters are experienced with carbon steel, stainless steel and alloy pipelines used in water, steam, compressed air, chemical and process applications. We follow international welding procedures and inspection standards, including hydro testing and non-destructive testing, to guarantee leak-free and durable piping systems. Trust our team for plant piping, utility lines, pipe racks and maintenance piping projects delivered safely and within budget.

Why Choose Our Piping Services

Benefits of Professional Industrial Piping

Piping is the lifeline of any industrial plant - it carries the fluids, gases and process media that keep production running. Poor fabrication or weak joints can lead to leaks, downtime, environmental hazards and expensive repairs. With ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions, you get industrial piping built to code, tested to pressure and installed by a disciplined, safety-first team.

  • Certified Welding: SMAW and GTAW welding performed by qualified welders with documented quality control and weld inspection.
  • Quality Materials: We use certified carbon steel and stainless steel pipe, flanges and fittings from reputable suppliers.
  • Workshop Fabrication: Pipe spools are prefabricated in our workshop for better accuracy, faster erection and cleaner site work.
  • Hydrotested Lines: Every fabricated system is hydrostatically tested to the required pressure to guarantee leak-tight performance.
  • Complete Engineering: We provide isometric drawings, spool drawings and material take-offs for every piping project.
  • HSE Compliance: All work is executed under strict safety rules with proper permits, PPE and housekeeping standards.

Whether you need a new process line, plant expansion, replacement of corroded piping or complete piping for a greenfield project, our team delivers on schedule with full documentation. We work in tight industrial environments every day, planning lifts, shutdowns and tie-ins with minimal disruption to your operations.

Our Process

How We Deliver Your Piping Project

1

Survey & Detailing

We survey the site, review process requirements and prepare isometric and spool drawings with material take-off.

2

Fabrication

Frequently Asked Questions

Industrial Piping FAQs

  • What is industrial piping fabrication?
    Industrial piping fabrication is the process of cutting, beveling, fitting and welding pipe spools and complete piping systems for industrial plants. It covers carbon steel and stainless steel pipe, flanges, fittings and supports used to carry fluids, gases and process media.
  • Which materials do you work with for piping?
    We fabricate and install carbon steel and stainless steel piping systems, including process piping, pipe spools, flanges and fittings, with welding performed using SMAW and GTAW processes to meet project specifications.
  • Do you provide welding and hydrotesting services?
    Yes. Our certified welders perform SMAW and GTAW welding with full quality control, and every fabricated line is hydrotested to the required pressure before commissioning to prove strength and leak-tightness.
  • Can you fabricate pipe spools for industrial plants?
    Yes. We fabricate pipe spools in our workshop with precise dimensional control and then transport them to site for installation, which improves quality, speeds up erection and reduces on-site work.
  • What industries do you supply piping systems to?
    We serve power plants, oil and gas facilities, refineries, chemical plants, textile mills, cement plants, food processing units and general industrial facilities across Pakistan.
  • Which cities do you serve for piping installation?
    We provide industrial piping fabrication and installation in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all major industrial cities across Pakistan.
