Why Choose Our PEB Steel Structures

Benefits of Pre-Engineered Buildings

PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) technology has become the preferred construction method for industrial and commercial buildings across Pakistan. Compared to conventional buildings, PEB structures offer significant advantages that save time, money and maintenance cost over the life of the building.

  • Fast Construction: Components are factory-made, so erection on site is 30-40% faster than conventional buildings.
  • Cost Effective: Lower material usage and reduced labour cost mean better value for every rupee.
  • Quality Control: Factory fabrication ensures consistent welding, tolerances and material quality.
  • Large Clear Spans: PEB allows clear spans up to 90m without internal columns, maximising usable space.
  • Flexible & Expandable: Structures can be easily extended later with minimal disruption.
  • Weather Resistant: Proper design and cladding protect your assets from wind, rain and heat.

Every project is executed with a dedicated team of engineers, certified welders, riggers and erectors working under strict HSE rules and quality standards. We deliver on time, on budget, and with complete documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

PEB Steel Structure FAQs

  • What is a PEB steel structure?
    A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a steel building designed, fabricated and assembled using standard structural members produced in a factory and erected on site. It offers faster construction, lower cost and better quality control than conventional buildings.
  • What is the cost of a PEB steel structure in Pakistan?
    The cost depends on the span, height, cladding, insulation and site location. Contact us with your requirements for a free, detailed quotation and budget estimation.
  • How long does installation take?
    A typical PEB structure is erected within 4 to 10 weeks depending on size and complexity. Our teams work to strict schedules to ensure on-time delivery.
  • Do you provide design and drawing support?
    Yes. Our engineering team provides complete design, structural calculations, shop drawings and erection drawings for every project.
  • Which cities do you serve?
    We provide services in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan and all major industrial cities across Pakistan.
