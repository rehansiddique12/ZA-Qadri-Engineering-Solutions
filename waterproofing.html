Waterproofing Services in Pakistan

Water leakage damages structures, equipment and stock, and the longer it is ignored the costlier it becomes. ZA Qadri Engineering Solutions provides reliable waterproofing services in Pakistan for roofs, terraces, tanks, basements and industrial floors.

We apply advanced liquid-applied membranes, cementitious coatings and sheet membranes using proper surface preparation and detailed work at joints, parapets and pipe penetrations. Our waterproofing systems are weather-resistant, long-lasting and applied by trained crews with a strong focus on thorough workmanship. Protect your building and its contents - contact us for a roof and terrace waterproofing inspection in Lahore and across Pakistan.

Our waterproofing specialists also serve industrial roofs, machine foundations, pump rooms and water treatment areas where moisture control is critical. With a structured guarantee on workmanship and the right system for every surface, we help you avoid costly repairs. Get a free site survey and waterproofing quotation from our Lahore office today, and let our specialists recommend the most durable solution for your roof, terrace or industrial floor.

Why Choose Our Waterproofing

Benefits of Professional Waterproofing

Water is the biggest enemy of any building. Without proper waterproofing, rainwater seeps through shades roofs and terraces, causes damp patches, rusts steel sheets, damages stored goods and shortens the life of the entire structure. A professionally applied waterproofing system is the most cost-effective protection you can invest in.

  • Permanent Leak Protection: Seamless membranes stop leaks at the source instead of temporary patches.
  • Structural Safety: Protection from water keeps concrete, masonry and steel strong for many years.
  • UV & Weather Resistant: Quality coatings withstand sunlight, rain, heat and temperature changes.
  • Mould & Dampness Control: Prevents damp walls, fungus and musty odours inside your building.
  • Cost Saving: One-time treatment avoids repeated leak repairs and major structural damage later.
  • Expert Application: Proper surface preparation, priming and coating layers applied by trained teams.

Whether you own an industrial shades, a factory roof, a commercial building or a home, our team provides a complete solution from inspection and leak identification to surface repair, priming, coating and final testing. We serve residential, commercial and industrial clients with the same commitment to quality and durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Waterproofing FAQs

  • What areas can you waterproof?
    We waterproof shades roofs, terraces, rooftops, water tanks, bathrooms, kitchens, basements, swimming pools and complete industrial and commercial buildings across Pakistan.
  • What types of waterproofing materials do you use?
    We use high-performance polyurethane coatings, acrylic coatings, cementitious waterproofing systems and membrane-based solutions selected to match your surface, exposure and budget.
  • How long does waterproofing last?
    With proper surface preparation and quality material, our waterproofing systems provide durable protection for 5 to 10 years or more depending on the coating type and service conditions.
  • Can you fix existing roof leaks?
    Yes. We locate the source of the leak, repair the cracks, joints and damaged areas, and then apply a complete waterproofing layer to prevent future leakage.
  • Do you waterproof water tanks and bathrooms?
    Yes. We waterproof potable and storage water tanks and provide complete bathroom and wet-area waterproofing to keep water in place and protect the structure from seepage and dampness.
  • Which cities do you serve?
    We provide waterproofing services in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all major cities across Pakistan for residential, commercial and industrial buildings.
