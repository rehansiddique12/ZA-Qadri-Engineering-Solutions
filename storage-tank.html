Why Choose Our Storage Tanks

Benefits of Professional Tank Fabrication

Storage tanks are critical assets. A poorly fabricated tank can cause leakage, product loss, environmental damage and even serious safety hazards. Our fabrication process follows recognized standards and strict quality checks at every stage, giving you tanks that perform reliably for decades.

  • Certified Materials: Tanks are built from tested carbon steel and stainless steel plates with material certificates.
  • Code-Based Design: Fabrication follows API, ASME and international standards for strength and safety.
  • Qualified Welding: Approved weld procedures and certified welders ensure leak-tight, durable joints.
  • Hydrostatic Testing: Every tank is water-tested to verify strength, seams and complete leak-tightness.
  • Corrosion Protection: Sandblasting and painting protect the tank surface from rust and chemical attack.
  • Complete Turnkey Service: Design, fabrication, erection, piping and testing under one contract.

Whether you need a fuel storage tank at a petrol station, an oil tank for an industrial plant, a water reservoir for a housing scheme or a chemical tank for a processing unit, our team delivers with precision, on schedule and in full compliance with HSE rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Storage Tank Fabrication FAQs

  • What types of storage tanks do you fabricate?
    We fabricate carbon steel and stainless steel storage tanks for fuel and oil, potable and industrial water, chemicals and process fluids, including API/ASME style tanks, bulk storage tanks and field-erected tanks of various capacities.
  • Do you fabricate tanks to API and ASME standards?
    Yes. Our storage tanks are designed and fabricated following recognized standards such as API 650 and ASME, using certified materials, approved welding procedures and qualified welders to ensure structural integrity and safety.
  • What materials are used for tank fabrication?
    We use certified carbon steel, such as ASTM A36 and pressure vessel grades, and stainless steel such as SS 304 and SS 316, depending on the stored product, ensuring corrosion resistance and long service life.
  • How do you test storage tanks before delivery?
    Every tank is subjected to hydrostatic testing to verify strength and leak-tightness, along with weld inspections, dimensional checks and internal and external coating and painting as per specification.
  • Do you provide tank erection and installation in Pakistan?
    Yes. We provide shell and roof erection, nozzle and appurtenance installation, piping tie-ins, hydrostatic testing and sandblasting and painting across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and all of Pakistan.
  • Can you fabricate custom-size storage tanks?
    Yes. We fabricate tanks to custom diameters, heights and capacities based on your process requirement and site constraints, with complete design and drawing support from our engineering team.
